Hello! I'm

Samantha Omer.

Engineering at the microscale.

I design, fabricate and validate microscale devices in San Diego, from photomask to cleanroom prototype, with a focus on microfluidics, MEMS and electroporation for therapeutic delivery.

I studied Biotechnology at Cal Poly Pomona and Bioengineering at San Diego State, where my thesis at NanoFAB.SDSU used a continuous-flow microfluidic chip to deliver genes into living cells. I love the whole loop, from mask design to cell assays, and teaching it to the next group of researchers.
